A template is an ordinary note subtree that happens to live under **Templates** in the tree. Anything you can write in a note, you can put in a template - headings, checklists, tables, child notes, attachments.

## Try it

There is a **Project** template already set up, with Overview, Ideas, and Log underneath it. To use it:

1. Press the template icon at the top of the tree, or right-click any note and choose **Add from template**.
2. Pick **Project** and give the new note a name.
3. The whole subtree is copied into place - root, children, and any attachments - with fresh ids of its own.

The copy is a normal note from that moment on. Editing it never touches the template, and editing the template never touches copies you already made.

## Making your own

Drag any note into the Templates section and it becomes a template, children and all. Drag it back out and it is a normal note again. That is the whole mechanism - there is nothing else to learn, and no separate template editor.

## What they are good for

- A meeting note with the same headings every time
- A weekly review checklist
- A per-project skeleton like the Project template
- A structured brief you hand to an agent to fill in
